Squizzcap - Changelog
=====================


V1.0.0
------

First public release.

- Pops up a detailed death recap when you die, built from Blizzard's own
  death recap data.
- The window stays open through release, resurrection and zoning until you
  close it.
- Shows the killing blow, total damage taken, damage by source broken down by
  spell, and a list of the hits leading up to your death.
- /squizzcap test shows your last death again; /squizzcap (or /scr) opens the
  options.
